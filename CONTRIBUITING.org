* Contributing to the Plum

Thanks for even thinking about contributing. That's already more than most people do.

** Before you do anything

I really want to keep this project *stupid and simple*.
I'm not Suckless and I don't to be, but don't want this to turn into a big complicated thing that becomes painful to maintain.

So please keep this in mind:
+ Prefer small and focused changes.
+ Don't add features just because other SSG have them.
+ If your idea makes the code significantly more complex, think twice (or just ask first).

** Reporting issues

Found a bug? Something doesn't work?
Open an issue and tell me:
+ What you did
+ What you expected
+ What actually happened

That's enough.

** Pull Requests

PRs are welcome.

A few simple rules:
1. Keep it simple.
2. Explain *why* the change is needed (not just what it does).
3. Try not to break the *"stupid"* part of Plum.

If you're not sure whether your idea fits the project, just open an issue first and ask.

** About using AI

Using AI in any form is *strongly discouraged*, with only two exceptions:

1. For communication, talking, and translation.
2. When you fully understand what the LLM wrote and can explain it yourself if asked.

If you don't follow these rules, your contribution may be closed or removed.
In repeated or obvious cases (junk PRs, low-effort AI-generated code, etc.), you may also be banned from the project.

Your own words and your own code are more than enough for this project.
We're here to learn, write code, and talk to each other, not to have a machine in the middle.

** Code style

+ Scheme side: keep it readable. Don't be clever for the sake of being clever.
+ C side (the FFI/shim parts): keep it minimal and clear.
+ Don't introduce big abstractions unless they actually make things simpler.
